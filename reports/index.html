What is not established

  • No precision or recall for any rule: there is no labelled ground truth. The grammar fixes the vocabulary; only ground truth can score the detections.
  • No real camera input in these runs; the footage is simulation.
  • Runs are 60 to 390 seconds, not sustained operation.
  • Sixty frames of one video is a signal about the 8B, not a rate.
